Privacy Policy

By Michael

In short: this website does not track you. There are no analytics cookies, no marketing cookies and no advertising trackers on Flapjack Recipe.

What this website collects

Nothing, in the ordinary run of things. You can read every page, use the timers and scale every recipe without an account, and without the site recording who you are. We do not run analytics software, we do not embed advertising, and we do not sell or share visitor data — there is no visitor data to sell.

What is stored in your own browser

Two features remember things on your own device only, using your browser's local storage: the cooking timers (so they survive a page refresh) and your cookie-choice setting (so we do not ask on every visit). This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply and to run the site properly. The contact form on this site does not store or transmit anything itself — it opens your own email app, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

Third-party services

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google's servers; Google may log that request in the ordinary way web servers do. Photographs credited to their photographers are hosted on this site itself. Pages may link out to other websites (such as our sister site, Cottage Pie Recipe, or public food-safety guidance); those sites have their own privacy policies, and this one does not cover them.

Your rights

If you have emailed us and want your messages deleted, write to flapjackrecipe@gmail.com and we will delete them. Because the site itself collects no visitor data, there is no account or tracking profile to request or erase.

Changes to this policy

If the way this site works ever changes — for example, if analytics are ever added — this page will be updated to say so plainly before any new tracking begins.

Questions people ask
