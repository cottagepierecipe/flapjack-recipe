Terms of Use

By Michael

The short version: read the recipes, cook them, print them, share the links. Do not republish the site's words or photographs as your own.

Using the recipes

Everything on Flapjack Recipe is free to read and use in your own kitchen. You may print recipes for personal use and share links to any page — sharing links is always welcome and never needs permission.

Copying content

The text on this site is written for this site. Please do not republish articles, recipe text or substantial parts of pages on other websites, in apps or in print without asking first via the contact page. A short quotation with a clear link back is fine, as it is anywhere.

Photographs and their licences

Photographs on this site are real photographs. Some are the site's own; others are used under free licences (such as Creative Commons) and are credited to their photographers in the caption, as those licences require. A photo's licence travels with the photo: our use of a credited photograph does not give anyone else the right to reuse it without checking its licence at its source, which the credit identifies.

Accuracy

We work hard to keep recipes and advice accurate, and we fix mistakes when they are pointed out. Even so, the content is provided as guidance, and the disclaimer explains the limits — in particular around ovens varying, allergies and nutrition.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to outside websites, including our sister site Cottage Pie Recipe and public food-safety guidance. We are not responsible for the content of other people's sites.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the updated version will be published on this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means the new terms apply to that use.
