Contact Flapjack Recipe

By Michael

Questions about a recipe, a correction, or an idea for a guide — the quickest way to reach us is email.

Email us

Write to flapjackrecipe@gmail.com. Every message is read. Recipe questions are answered fastest when you say which recipe you made and what happened — "my flapjacks came out hard" plus the page name is enough to start.

Or prepare an email here

Fill in this short form and press the button. Nothing is sent by this website: the form simply opens your own email app with your message addressed and written out, ready for you to check and press send yourself. Until you press send in your own app, nothing has been sent — and this website stores nothing you type here.

Your email app should now be open with your message prepared and addressed to us. Please press send in your email app to deliver it — this website has not sent anything.
