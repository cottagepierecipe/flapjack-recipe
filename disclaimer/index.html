Disclaimer

By Michael

The recipes and guides on Flapjack Recipe are written with care, but they are guidance for home cooking — your kitchen, your oven and your judgement are part of every recipe.

Recipes are guidance

Ovens vary, ingredients vary, and tins vary. Times and temperatures in our recipes are given in good faith with the cues to watch for — colour, texture and set — because those cues matter more than the clock. If your oven runs hot or your bars colour faster than the recipe suggests, trust what you see.

Tested and draft recipes

Pages say plainly whether a recipe has been kitchen-tested by us or is a draft worked out from cross-checked published sources. Draft recipes are written to be cooked, but their times and results have not yet been confirmed in our kitchen, and the page will not pretend otherwise.

Nutrition

We do not publish nutrition figures unless they have been calculated from a specific, named recipe using the values on the ingredient labels, and any figure shown is labelled as a calculation, not a measurement. Nothing on this site is medical or dietary advice. If you are cooking for a medical condition — diabetes, coeliac disease or any other — talk to your clinician or dietitian about what suits you, and always check the labels of the ingredients you buy.

Allergies and ingredients

Recipes list their ingredients in full, but manufacturers change recipes and oats in particular are often milled alongside wheat. If you are cooking for someone with an allergy or coeliac disease, checking the labels of the actual packets in your kitchen is your responsibility — a website cannot do that for you.

Food safety

Storage advice on this site follows mainstream UK guidance for home kitchens. Use your judgement with leftovers and keeping times, store food as the recipes advise, and if in doubt, throw it out. Babies under 12 months must not be given honey, including in baking.
