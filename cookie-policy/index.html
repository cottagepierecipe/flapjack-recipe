Cookie Policy & Settings

By Michael

This website keeps things simple: it sets no analytics cookies, no marketing cookies and no advertising cookies. What little the site remembers is stored in your own browser, on your own device.

What this site stores, and why

WhatWhereWhy
Cooking timersYour browser's local storageSo a running timer survives a page refresh while you cook.
Essential serving cookies (hosting)Set by the site's hosting, if at allOnly what is technically needed to deliver pages securely. No tracking.

None of this is used to follow you around the internet, build a profile or target advertising. There is no advertising on this site.

Your settings

There is no cookie notice on this site, because there is nothing to consent to: the site sets no tracking, analytics or marketing cookies. The only thing stored is your cooking timers, in your own browser on your own device. You can clear them at any time through your browser's settings (look for "site data" or "clear browsing data").

Third parties

The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them. That is a font download, not a cookie set by this site. If this ever changes, this page will say so.

Questions people ask
